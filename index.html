Dance, Fitness, and Kids Programs in San Antonio

SMASH Dance Studio is a welcoming boutique dance and fitness studio in San Antonio, Texas, offering fun classes for all ages and skill levels and a positive and supportive environment. Founded in Las Vegas in 2005 by former performer Lisa Valdes, SMASH moved to San Antonio in 2010 and quickly became a vibrant part of the local dance community.

We stand out for our wide variety of dance and fitness classes that bring families together while promoting kindness, health, and confidence. From adult fitness to creative kids programs, every student learns to express themselves through movement while gaining strength and self-esteem.

Through our Project SMASH Community Outreach program, we share the joy of dance with those who may not otherwise have access, teaching the importance of giving back and staying active. Joining SMASH means becoming part of a positive San Antonio dance community that celebrates growth, creativity, and connection.

Why Choose SMASH Dance

Real Community Energy

SMASH Dance is more than a studio. It’s a San Antonio family where everyone supports each other’s growth, confidence, and creativity.

Classes for All Ages and Levels

Whether you’re a first-time dancer or returning after years, our programs for kids and adults meet every experience level with patience and excitement.

Empowering Instructors

Our passionate team brings years of dance and fitness experience to every class. They teach with encouragement, not intimidation, helping every student shine.

Fun and Judgment-Free Environment

We keep classes positive, inclusive, and full of energy. You’ll sweat, smile, and leave every class feeling better than when you arrived.

Diverse Programs and Events

From hip hop and heels to kids ballet and Zumba, we offer something for everyone. Plus, special events like bachelorette parties and community workshops keep things fresh.

Strong Local Roots

Proudly based in San Antonio, we partner with local schools, nonprofits, and organizations through Project SMASH to make dance accessible for everyone.

Affordable and Flexible Options

We make dance accessible with drop-in classes, multi-class packs, and unlimited memberships that fit every schedule and budget.

Results You Can Feel

Our classes improve fitness, posture, confidence and mindset helping you feel stronger inside and out.
