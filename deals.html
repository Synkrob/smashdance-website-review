LET’S CELEBRATE

Two Decades of Passion, Purpose, and Dance

For twenty years, SMASH Dance Studio has been more than a place to learn dance. It has been a home for creativity, connection, and growth. What began in Las Vegas has evolved into a vibrant community in San Antonio, Texas, where our passion for dance continues to inspire generations.

From our early days performing in Las Vegas to creating programs for local schools, after-school activities, and community centers, every step has been driven by the love of teaching and the joy of movement. Our journey has touched countless families and dancers who found confidence, strength, and belonging through dance.

As we celebrate this milestone, we reflect with gratitude on every student, parent, and supporter who has been part of our story. Thank you, San Antonio, for embracing SMASH and helping us grow into the family we are today. We look forward to the next chapter, continuing to share our passion for dance and giving back to the community that has supported us every step of the way.
