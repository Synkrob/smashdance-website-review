Why Choose SMASH Dance

What types of adult classes do you offer?

We offer a wide range of classes for adults including Hip Hop, Zumba, Heels, Strength Training, Cardio Dance, and Stretch and Tone. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced dancer, there is a class that fits your pace and goals.

Do I need any dance experience to join?

Not at all. Our adult classes are designed for all fitness levels and abilities. Instructors break down moves step by step, making sure everyone feels comfortable and confident from start to finish.

How can I register or try a class?

We recommend trying a class first to see which style you enjoy most. You can drop in for a single session or register for a full pass once you find your favorite class. Registration is quick and can be completed online or at the studio.

What should I bring or wear to class?

Wear comfortable, breathable workout clothes and supportive shoes. Bring a water bottle and a towel if needed. Most classes are high energy, so hydration is important.

Do you offer memberships or class packages?

Yes. We offer flexible class passes and memberships to fit your schedule. You can purchase a single class, a 10 class pass, or a SMASH VIP Unlimited Membership for full access to all adult fitness and dance classes.

Can I take multiple classes in one day?

Absolutely. Many members enjoy combining cardio and dance with strength or flexibility training. Our schedule allows you to mix and match classes throughout the week to meet your fitness goals.

What makes SMASH Dance different from other studios in San Antonio?

SMASH Dance is a welcoming boutique studio that focuses on community, confidence, and creativity. Our instructors bring years of professional experience while keeping every class fun, supportive, and encouraging. We have built a space where everyone can move, laugh, and grow together in the heart of San Antonio, Texas.
