Our Story

From an early age, founder Lisa believed dance had the power to build confidence, self-esteem, and opportunity. As a single mother at 17, she wanted her daughter to experience those same benefits but struggled to find affordable dance classes. After building a successful dance studio, she never forgot those challenges and created Project SMASH to help make dance, education, and fitness accessible to others facing similar obstacles.

Our Vision

Empowering communities by fostering happy, healthy, and productive lives. We provide underprivileged children, at-risk youth, and women in recovery with access to dance, fitness, education, food distribution, and creative opportunities throughout San Antonio and Las Vegas.

Our Values

Creating communities where every individual has the opportunity to discover their potential, build confidence, and achieve a brighter future through greater access to education, creativity, wellness, meaningful opportunities, and lifelong personal growth.

Project SMASH in Action

Our Programs

Our programs are designed to strengthen individuals and communities by fostering confidence, promoting healthy lifestyles, encouraging creativity, and creating opportunities for lifelong personal growth through education, dance, fitness, and community engagement.

Dance to Heal

We partner with children’s homes and recovery facilities to provide dance and fitness programs that promote healing, build confidence, encourage healthy lifestyles, and support personal growth through movement.

SMASH Kosmic

Our K-pop performance team develops dance technique, leadership, teamwork, and confidence while performing throughout the community and inspiring youth through creativity, service, and personal growth.

Dance Scholarships

We provide need-based scholarships that make dance education accessible to children and families, ensuring financial barriers never prevent students from discovering their talents and reaching their potential.

Apply for a Dance Scholarship

Stock Playground

This Las Vegas program combines dance and financial literacy to teach youth practical money management skills while encouraging confidence, leadership, teamwork, and lifelong learning.

Food Distribution

Project SMASH partners with the San Antonio Food Bank to help provide nutritious food to individuals and families throughout our community. Regular food distribution events help local residents access essential groceries and resources.
