SMASH DANCE

Kids Dance Program in San Antonio

At SMASH Dance Studio in San Antonio, we help kids ages 3–17 discover confidence, creativity, and joy through movement. Our classes welcome beginners through experienced young dancers in a fun and encouraging space. Students can explore a variety of styles including Ballet, Jazz, Hip Hop, Breakdance, Musical Theatre, Pom, K Pop, Acro, and Zumba Kids. Our Kosmic Kpop Dance Team gives passionate young dancers a chance to perform and grow as part of a close-knit group. Every year, our students take the stage for two showcases that raise funds for local charities, teaching the importance of community and kindness through dance. At SMASH, we believe every child deserves a place to move, express themselves, and be part of something special.

Why Choose SMASH Dance

What age groups do you offer kids dance classes for?

Our kids programs start as young as age 3 and continue through teens. Each class is designed to match the student’s age, skill level, and creativity, helping them grow in confidence and coordination.

What styles of dance can my child learn at SMASH Dance?

We offer a variety of styles including Hip Hop, Ballet, Jazz, Contemporary, Tumbling, and K-Pop. Our instructors focus on building strong foundations while keeping each class fun, active, and engaging.

How can I register my child for a class?

We always encourage parents to let their child try a class first to see if they enjoy it and feel comfortable in the environment. Once they find the class that fits them best, registration can easily be completed online or at the studio.

What should my child wear to class?

Comfortable, fitted clothing that allows movement works best. Sneakers or dance shoes are recommended depending on the class. Avoid jeans or loose jewelry for safety and mobility.

Do kids perform during the year?

Yes. Our students participate in two showcases each year, giving them the opportunity to perform on stage for family and friends while supporting local charities through our Project SMASH community outreach program.

What is the Kosmic K-Pop Dance Team?

Kosmic is our exciting K-Pop performance group for kids and teens who love high-energy choreography and teamwork. Members train together to learn iconic routines and perform at local San Antonio events and showcases. It’s a great way to build confidence, stage presence, and lifelong friendships.

Affordable and Flexible Options

We make dance accessible with drop-in classes, multi-class packs, and unlimited memberships that fit every schedule and budget.

Can I see my kids in class?

No, parents are not allowed in class during regular sessions. However, parents are welcome to stay for the first class until their child feels comfortable. After that, parents can watch a live stream of the class directly on their phone through our app, giving them peace of mind while allowing kids to stay focused and independent in class.
