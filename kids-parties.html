What is a Kids Dance Party?

Kids dance class parties at SMASH offer a fun, structured dance experience led by professional instructors. Each event includes a guided 40-minute dance lesson with age-appropriate choreography in a supportive studio environment. No prior dance experience is required.

After the lesson, kids enjoy additional celebration time with music, lights, and space to relax and socialize. These events are supervised and designed to keep kids engaged while giving parents peace of mind. Dance class parties are available upon request and are ideal for birthdays and small group celebrations for children ages 6 and up.

Policies

Please review the policies below to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience. These guidelines help keep our studio safe, clean, and comfortable for everyone.

Cleaning Fee

The room must be left in the same condition it was found, with all trash placed in the alley bin. A $100 cleaning fee will apply if the room is left messy. This fee may be paid in advance or charged to the card on file after the event if cleaning is required.

Children

Children must be ages 6 and up to be allowed in the room. No small children are permitted. All children must be supervised by adults at all times, and children must stay off the mirrors and walls.

Responsibilities

The renter must sign a waiver and is responsible for any damage that occurs during the event.

Food

Only pizza, cake, or small finger foods are allowed. A $100 cleaning fee will apply if the room is not left in the same condition it was found.

Set up/Clean up

Renter can arrive 25 minutes before to set up and has 25 minutes after the event to clean up.
