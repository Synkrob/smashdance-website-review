Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before planning your SMASH celebration.

Do I need dance experience to participate?

No dance experience is required. All sessions are instructor-led and designed to be approachable for all skill levels.

What is the difference between a class-style session and a Divas Night Out?

A class-style session focuses on learning choreography in a fitness-style setting for 60 minutes. A Divas Night Out format runs about 90 minutes and combines guided choreography with a more social, night-out atmosphere.

Are heels required?

Heels are optional. Some themes are designed for heels, but participants are welcome to dance without them.

Can we choose our own theme?

Yes. Groups can select from available themes and choose the experience that best fits their event.

Are the dance routines beginner-friendly?

Yes. Choreography is designed to be fun, engaging, and accessible for beginners.

Can participants dance without heels?

Yes. Participants may dance in heels, sneakers, or other comfortable footwear depending on the theme.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable clothing you can move in. Fitness attire is recommended for class-style sessions, while themed or night-out attire works well for social party formats.

Can I dance barefoot or in sneakers?

Yes. Sneakers are recommended for non-heels classes, and barefoot participation is allowed depending on the theme.

What types of events are these parties best for?

Adult dance parties are great for birthdays, bachelorette celebrations, girls’ nights, team events, and group outings.

Is there a minimum or maximum group size?

Yes. A minimum of 7 participants is required to book, though the coordinator can still pay the difference if fewer than 7 attend. The maximum group size is 30 participants, depending on room availability and setup.

How do I request an adult dance party?

Adult dance parties are available upon request. Please contact us directly to discuss availability, themes, and group size.

How far in advance should I book?

Booking at least two to three weeks in advance is recommended to ensure availability, especially for larger groups.
